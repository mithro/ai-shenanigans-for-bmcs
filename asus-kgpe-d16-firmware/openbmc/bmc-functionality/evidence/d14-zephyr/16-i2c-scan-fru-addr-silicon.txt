CONCLUSION (ground truth from silicon):
  * The board FRU EEPROM (U25, Holtek HT24LC08, 8 Kbit) responds at 0x54-0x57 and
    NOT at 0x50-0x53. A 24C08 has one external address pin (A2) + 2 internal block
    bits; ACKing 0x54-0x57 while NAKing 0x50-0x53 means A2/E2 is strapped to VCC.
  * Therefore the Zephyr dts `fru_eeprom@54` (reg = <0x54>) is HARDWARE-CORRECT.
    DTS/Kconfig-review Finding 2 ("dts 0x54 wrong, should be 0x50 per schematic") is
    REFUTED by the hardware — the schematic's 0x50-0x53 is the unstrapped base range,
    not the address this board actually presents.
  * This also confirms the earlier fru_smoke PASS at 0x54 was a REAL ACK, not a
    NAK-misread: the i2c_aspeed_g3 master returns -ENXIO on an address NAK
    (verified here: 0x50-0x53 -> ret=-6 = -ENXIO), and 0x54-0x57 returned 0.
  * QEMU and silicon AGREE exactly (QEMU run: same 0x50-53 NAK / 0x54-57 ACK), so
    the QEMU kgpe-d16-bmc FRU model is faithful to the real board address.

Cross-checks the SCU74[12] I2C5 pin-mux fix (#156) once more: the master reaches the
bus and gets per-address ACK/NAK — the engine-4 pinmux is working on silicon.
