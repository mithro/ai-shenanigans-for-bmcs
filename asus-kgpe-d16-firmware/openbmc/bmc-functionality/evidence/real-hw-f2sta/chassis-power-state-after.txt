--- 2. PROOF op-pwrctl startup is READ-ONLY to host power (openbmc/skeleton source) ---
op-pwrctl set_up_gpio(): NO gpio_write() at startup; only
  gpio_read(&power_gpio->power_good_in, &pgood_state); control_power_set_pgood(...);
Outputs are driven ONLY in on_set_power_state() (explicit setPowerState) or on a pgood
TRANSITION in poll_pgood (`if (pgood_state != current)`). With pgood stable at 1 and no
power command, restarting the daemon drives nothing.  => safe on the running host.
