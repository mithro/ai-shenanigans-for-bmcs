Root cause of the earlier "PMBus status register not found" failure
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Self-inflicted device-tree mis-nesting, NOT a model or hardware fault.
The first attempt placed `psu@58` under the node textually written `i2c@0`
that is a CHILD of the engine-1 PCA954x mux (`i2c-parent = <&i2c1>`, QU5
channel Y0) — a mux child bus, not the engine-0 controller. The QEMU PSU
lives on the engine-0 DIRECT bus, so on the mux child every read NAK'd and
the generic driver's STATUS_WORD/STATUS_BYTE identify (pmbus_core.c
pmbus_init_common) returned -ENXIO -> "status register not found".
Fix: attach the node to the controller by its label `&i2c0` (a new top-level
block), matching the QEMU engine-0 placement. Trap avoided: an Aspeed board
dts can have two different `i2c@0` nodes — the SoC controller (label &i2c0)
and a mux child (reg = <0> under a mux). They are different buses.

Result: the generic pmbus driver identifies + binds and reads live model
telemetry (VIN 230 V, VOUT 12 V, temp 30 C, IIN 1 A, IOUT 8 A). The -500
entries are absent channels (VCAP/temp2/temp3) the driver probes but the
PSU model does not populate — harmless.
