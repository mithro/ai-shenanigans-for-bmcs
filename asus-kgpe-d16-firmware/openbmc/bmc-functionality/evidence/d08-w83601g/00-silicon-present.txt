D08 W83601G DIMM-LED I2C GPIO expanders (U27/U28) on silicon, 2026-07-18.
Both respond on the BMC's direct I2C5 engine (Linux i2c-4), confirming the
schematic §10.2 wiring:

  # i2cget -y 4 0x18 0x00  -> 0x0f
  # i2cget -y 4 0x18 0x01  -> 0x00
  # i2cget -y 4 0x18 0x07  -> 0xff
  # i2cget -y 4 0x19 0x00  -> 0xb5

Findings:
- U27 (0x18) and U28 (0x19) are both PRESENT and register-readable by the BMC
  over I2C5. This validates the BMC's DIMM-error-LED expander access path on
  real silicon — the "5 far-end devices absent" audit gap is now down to
  SB-TSI / PSU-PMBus / SMBus-ALERT for the fully-modeled set.
- The BMC drives DIMM{A..H}ERRLED by writing the W83601G GPIO output registers
  (GP10-GP26, §10.2). Reading is non-destructive; the observed defaults above
  seed a QEMU model.
